# Clayday showcase: storylines

The one thing: your whole workday waiting for you as a cosy clay studio you play in every morning.

## A. A morning at the table (recommended)

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take across the studio.

It's 08:58 and the window is still dawn-pink. The camera sits down at the table and glides object to object without a cut. At each stop one real action happens: a Slack sticky gets 👀, a session lump opens in cmux, a PR piece dries on the rack, the sponge wipes 6 GB, the jar fills. Light turns from dawn to golden as the day gets done.

- First 2 s: dawn light on the empty table, a clock tag says 08:58, the kettle steam curls, and the rolling pin rolls everything in.
- Beats: dawn hook → roll to refresh → corkboard (pin, 👀, ✅) → work board (meetings) → cutting mat (sessions, open in cmux) → rack + market calendar (PRs waiting for deploy) → sketchbook (idea → Notion) → sponge (wipe) → jar fills, golden hour → Clayday sign end card.
- Look: the studio itself, painterly Ghibli light, handwritten Patrick Hand labels at 2× size, butter, rose and mint clay.
- Sound: soft acoustic lo-fi, with foley on every action: squish, pin pop, paper tear, sponge squeak, jar clink.

## B. Pinch, roll, wipe

Device: a character does the work. Form: cut on the beat.

A little clay hand is the cursor. Every object is one verb, written huge in handwriting and cut to the music: Pin. Roll. Dry. Wipe. Plop.

- First 2 s: the clay hand pokes the screen. "Pinch" lands, the lump squishes and the beat drops.
- Beats: hand intro → Pin (corkboard) → Roll (refresh) → Dry (rack) → Sketch (sketchbook) → Wipe (sponge) → Plop (jar) → sign end card.
- Look: flat butter-yellow colour fields between shots of the real studio, with giant handwritten verbs.
- Sound: bouncy ukulele beat, a squish on every cut.

## C. From chaos to clay (the bold one)

Device: life before and after. Form: an endless zoom.

The opening shows a frantic laptop: 40 tabs, red Slack badges, PR emails and terminal tabs. The rolling pin flattens it all into one soft lump of clay. Then the camera dives into the lump, which turns into the studio. Each object we zoom into turns out to be the next one.

- First 2 s: a wall of flashing notification badges. Then a rolling pin crosses the frame and leaves it flat clay.
- Beats: chaos → flattened → dive into the studio → corkboard → mat → rack → sponge → zoom out to the sign.
- Look: harsh cold UI greys for the chaos, then the warm studio. That contrast is the whole film.
- Sound: a rising stressful hum cut dead by one soft squish, then calm music.

Assumptions: 35–45 s, 1920×1080, music and sound effects, no voiceover. Footage is the real Clay Studio scene filled with made-up sample data (not your real Slack, PRs or sessions). Music is free library music; nothing paid.
